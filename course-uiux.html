Why Choose Our UI/UX & Web Program?

UI/UX designers are among the most sought-after professionals in tech. Our program covers the complete design process — from user research and wireframing to high-fidelity prototypes and front-end development — preparing you for roles at top startups and tech giants.

Curriculum

What You'll Learn

From user psychology to pixel-perfect design and coded front-ends — a comprehensive digital design education.

Module 01

Design Thinking & UX Research

User personas, journey maps, empathy mapping, competitor analysis & usability testing.

Module 02

Wireframing & Information Architecture

Low & high-fidelity wireframes, site maps, user flows, content strategy.

Module 03

Visual Design & Typography

Colour theory, grid systems, font pairing, visual hierarchy & brand identity design.

Module 04

Figma & Prototyping

Component libraries, auto-layout, interactive prototypes, developer handoff in Figma.

Module 05

HTML5, CSS3 & JavaScript

Responsive web design, flexbox, CSS Grid, basic JS interactivity & animation.

Portfolio & Career Prep

Build a 5-project portfolio, Behance/Dribbble profile, LinkedIn optimisation, interview prep.
