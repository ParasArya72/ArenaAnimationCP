Curriculum

What You'll Learn

A complete game development curriculum covering art, design, and engineering.

Module 01

Game Design Fundamentals

Game theory, mechanics, rules, player psychology, genre study & GDD (Game Design Document).

Module 02

Game Art & Environment Design

Concept art, low-poly modelling, PBR texturing, prop & environment creation in Maya & Blender.

Module 03

Character Design & Animation

Character modelling, rigging, skinning & game-ready animations in Maya and Mixamo.

Module 04

Unity Game Development

Unity Editor, physics, UI systems, lighting, mobile & PC game deployment.

Module 05

Unreal Engine 5 (Lumen & Nanite)

UE5 Blueprints, Lumen lighting, Nanite geometry, level streaming & AAA-quality visuals.

Module 06

AR/VR & Game Publishing

Meta Quest development, WebXR, App Store / Play Store submission & monetisation strategies.
