Curriculum

What You'll Learn

From visual fundamentals to broadcast-ready motion graphics — a complete creative education.

Module 01

Design Fundamentals & Colour Theory

Visual principles, composition, grid systems, typography, colour theory & design history.

Module 02

Adobe Photoshop & Image Editing

Photo retouching, compositing, digital painting, photo manipulation & creative effects.

Module 03

Adobe Illustrator & Vector Design

Logo creation, icon design, infographics, brand identity systems & packaging design.

Module 04

Print & Digital Layout Design

Brochures, magazines, posters, social media creatives & digital ad campaigns in InDesign.

Module 05

After Effects & Motion Graphics

Kinetic typography, logo animation, broadcast graphics, reels, explainer videos & VFX elements.

Module 06

Portfolio & Freelancing

Behance profile, client communication, pricing, invoicing & building a freelance design career.
