Curriculum

What You'll Learn

A comprehensive curriculum designed with industry professionals to ensure you're job-ready from day one.

Module 01

Fundamentals of Animation

12 principles of animation, timing, spacing, squash & stretch, anticipation, and follow-through.

Module 02

2D Digital Animation

Frame-by-frame animation, motion tweening, Adobe Animate, storyboarding & lip sync.

Module 03

3D Modelling & Sculpting

Polygon modeling, organic sculpting in ZBrush, UV mapping, and texture painting.

Module 04

Rigging & Character Setup

Skeleton creation, skinning, weight painting, IK/FK systems, facial rigging in Maya.

Module 05

Character Animation

Walk cycles, run cycles, acting performances, emotion-driven animation for film & games.

Module 06

Lighting, Rendering & Compositing

Arnold/V-Ray rendering, HDRI lighting, compositing in Nuke/After Effects, final delivery.
