Course Overview

Why Choose Our VFX Program?

Our VFX course takes you from zero to studio-ready artist. You'll learn the full VFX pipeline — from green screen keying and rotoscoping, to advanced simulations in Houdini and final compositing in Nuke, just like top VFX houses.

With project-based learning and mentorship from artists who've worked on major productions, you'll graduate with a showreel that gets you noticed by recruiters at DNEG, Red Chillies, and Prime Focus.

Curriculum

What You'll Learn

A production-pipeline curriculum that mirrors real-world VFX studio workflows.

Module 01

VFX Fundamentals & Colour Science

Colour spaces, log footage, LUTs, digital imaging science for VFX workflows.

Module 02

Rotoscoping & Paint

Precision roto tools in Nuke & Mocha, clean-plate generation, wire removal.

Module 03

Keying & Green Screen

Advanced keying with Keylight, spill suppression, edge refinement, IBK Colour.

Module 04

Motion Tracking & Match Move

2D/3D camera tracking in Nuke & PFTrack, object tracking, projections.

Module 05

Houdini FX Simulations

Fluid, smoke, fire, rigid body, cloth & particle simulations in Houdini.

Module 06

Compositing & Delivery

Multi-pass compositing in Nuke, colour grading, DCP & broadcast-ready output.
