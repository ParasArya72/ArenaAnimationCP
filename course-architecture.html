Why Choose Our Architecture & Interior Program?

Our Architecture & Interior Design course equips you with both design sensibility and technical visualization skills. From AutoCAD floor plans to photorealistic V-Ray renders, you'll master the complete workflow demanded by top architecture firms and interior design studios.

Curriculum

What You'll Learn

From drafting blueprints to photorealistic walkthroughs — master the complete architectural design workflow.

Module 01

AutoCAD 2D Drafting

Floor plans, elevations, sections, site plans, dimensioning & technical drawing standards.

Module 02

3D Architectural Modelling

Building modelling in 3ds Max & SketchUp — walls, floors, roofs, doors, windows & facades.

Module 03

Interior Design & Space Planning

Furniture layout, colour schemes, material boards, lighting design & mood boards.

Module 04

V-Ray & Corona Rendering

Photorealistic exterior & interior rendering, HDRI lighting, material libraries & post-production.

Module 05

Lumion Walkthrough Animation

Real-time architectural visualization, flythrough animations, landscape & environment.

Module 06

Portfolio & Client Presentation

Design presentation boards, client briefs, Behance portfolio and professional project documentation.
